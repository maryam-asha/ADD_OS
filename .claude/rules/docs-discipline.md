# Writing the decision records

These files exist so reasoning survives the change it justified.

- **Never overwrite reasoning when a decision changes.** Append the reversal *with what was
  given up* — see the IBM Plex → Noto Sans reversal in `PHASE-0-AUDIT.md` Q1. A future
  reader must see the trade-off, not assume the first answer was simply wrong.
- **Keep the survey that preceded a removal.** `PHASE-3-DEAD-CONTROLS.md` retains the
  survey behind each deleted control precisely because the deletion erases the evidence.
- **Record deviations as deviations.** Where the product and the Brand Guideline disagree,
  say so; nothing is "fixed" by inference.
- **Never quote a credential fragment**, even while documenting its removal.
- Each entry states: what the source says, what was found, what ADD OS did in the meantime,
  and what is needed — plus who owns the decision.
- Update the relevant record in the same change that alters behaviour.
